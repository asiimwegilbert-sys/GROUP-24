Week 3 Report - Operator Overloads Design
Overview
This week focused on designing operator overloads for vector arithmetic. While implementations are pending, placeholders were introduced to define the intended functionality.
Progress
•	Declared operator overloads for addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication, and dot product.
•	Added placeholder implementations throwing std::logic_error.
Demonstrations
•	Outlined mathematical definitions for each operation.
•	Drafted pseudocode for addition and dot product.
Reflection
Declaring operators early ensures clarity in design and allows team members to anticipate how vectors will interact in trajectory computations. This staged approach supports collaborative development.
